# Crested Butte Avalanche Center

The mission of the Crested Butte Avalanche Center is to provide daily snow, weather
and avalanche information and is committed to reducing the impacts of avalanches on
human life and infrastructure in the Gunnison Valley through partnerships, education
and community outreach.

**This GitHub organization does not publish avalanche forecasts.** For current
conditions, go to [cbavalanchecenter.org](https://cbavalanchecenter.org/).
